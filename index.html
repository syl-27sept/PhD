How PhD progress is calculated

Progress is calculated hierarchically. Individual task progress contributes to milestones, milestones contribute to goals, goals contribute to academic years, and the years contribute to your overall PhD progress.

Each year, goal, milestone and roadmap task has an importance weight. A weight of 3 gives an item three times the influence of an item weighted 1 within the same parent level.

A completed task contributes 100%, a task in progress contributes 50%, and a task not started contributes 0%. A milestone can also be marked as completed directly.
